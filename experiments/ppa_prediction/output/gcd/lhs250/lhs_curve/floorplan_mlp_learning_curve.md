## How many trajectories?

- **power:**; rank ρ≥0.7 by **~80** train; MLP R²≥0.3 by **~20** train; plateau ~**150**
- **area:**; rank ρ≥0.7 by **~80** train; MLP R²≥0.3 by **~80** train; plateau ~**150**
- **wns:**; rank ρ≥0.7 by **~40** train; MLP R²≥0.3 by **~40** train; plateau ~**80**
- **tns:**; rank ρ≥0.7 by **~60** train; MLP R²≥0.3 by **~60** train
- **Overall (GCD):** linear often wins until ~80–100 train; MLP needs **≥60–80** to stop overfitting, **~100–150** to plateau. For ibex with more variance, budget **150–250** LHS labels; FastPASE used **482–997** but that included RTL diversity.

**Caveat:** GCD floorplan labels barely move (~1% variance). Ibex should need similar or fewer samples for *ranking*, but more to hit low NMAE.
